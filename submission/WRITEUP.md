# The Gilded Ace · Eye in the Sky

[Public source repository](https://github.com/kavuruhasini03-hue/gilded-ace-royale-submission)

**We recommend not replacing any of the ten lowest-earning machines in July. Their low earnings come mostly from the Back Forty's lost foot traffic and from game settings, so we spend $10,500 on three tests: new signage for the Back Forty, a payout check on machine M-1158, and moving machine M-1184 to a busier spot to learn whether its weakness is the spot or the machine. We keep $89,500 in reserve.**

Over six months, the floor recorded $248.9M in coin-in and $23.5M in theoretical win. Actual win exceeded theo by $92,791 (0.55 standard deviations), while lost operating time cost an estimated $98,064 in theo. We compared machines after accounting for downtime, zone and game configuration, then separated the remaining machine effect from payout variation. Three machines are long-term replacement candidates, with none clearing the July economics.

Back Forty weakened during the walkway closure relative to the rest of the floor, with covered-hour traffic supporting the location mechanism. A post-closure gap versus January remained. Seasonal and other changes limit causal attribution. Over 61 May–June days, the preselected model averaged $51,129 in error (3.84%), versus $218,258 for the constant baseline. The later weekday-mean baseline led April ($40,715) and was close in May–June ($68,699), so events and holidays add modest accuracy.

Tracked play was concentrated. The top 1% by six-month theo accounted for 22.72% of tracked theo and 22.80% of tracked coin-in and netted −$3.5M. The top 10% accounted for 66.37% and 66.33%, respectively, and netted −$10.5M. Economic comp cost was 1.23% of tracked theo. The redesigned 60-day pilot could detect a 5.77% change, above the 2.5% funding threshold. Monitor reinvestment weekly and review it if cost exceeds 1.50% of tracked theo for two complete weeks. No arm targets heavy losers.

M-1158’s payout gap is unusual after multiple-comparison adjustment (p = 0.0015). We assign 34% to a fixable cause because accounting, a resolved issue, or a non-fixable cause could also explain the gap. The $1,200 inspection has a modeled net value of +$785 (−$682 to +$2,275 across scenarios). Verify the meter, settings and accounting. Move M-1184 to vacant slot L-07-3 in Center Pit. Compare 28 days before and after the move with Center Pit peers. Test whether signs close at least 25% of Back Forty’s traffic and expected-win gaps. Read both tests at the end of August.
