Keep the weekday forecast for planning. It uses the day of the week, holidays and announced events to estimate total daily wagers. We fit the models through April and checked them on the next 61 days. Using only announcements known by April 30, the weekday forecast missed by $51,129 per day (3.84%). That is about 77% less than the constant forecast. A version with a straight-line trend performed worse on April validation and on May and June.

Skip both technician packages. Theo is what the house expects to win given the wagers and game settings. Shorter waits would recover about $381 in July theo for overnight coverage or $131 for Saturday evening coverage. Either package costs $11,520. Repricing with the trend forecast still leaves both options far short.

Defer the comp pilot. The proposed trial can reliably detect a change of 5.77% over 60 days, but it needs to detect 2.5% or less to guide the decision. Review comp pricing if economic comp cost exceeds 1.50% of tracked theo for two consecutive complete gaming weeks.

Keep $98,800 in reserve after the $1,200 inspection. Spend more only if the M-1158 check confirms a repeatable problem that is worth fixing, or if a bank-position test or updated lease estimate makes a watchlist option pay for itself.
