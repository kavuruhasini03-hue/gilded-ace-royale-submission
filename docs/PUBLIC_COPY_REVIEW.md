## Verdict

**Keep $98,800 in reserve and inspect one machine.** Theoretical win, or theo, is what the house expects to win given the wagers and game settings. Start with that expectation before reacting to actual payouts.

- **Expected win captured** This compares expected win with the opportunity after allowing for downtime and a better floor location.
- **Coin-in** This is the total amount wagered in the selected period.
- **Actual hold** This is the share of wagers the house kept.
- **Expected hold** This is the share of wagers the house expected to keep.
- **Payout variation** This is actual house win minus expected house win.
- **Expected win lost to downtime** We estimate the missed win using demand at the hours the machines were down.
- **Comp reinvestment** This divides the floor’s comp cost by expected win from tracked players for the selected dates.
- **Payout surprise score** This shows how far actual win was from expected win in units of typical variation. Scores between −2 and 2 are common.
- **July budget held in reserve** This is the cash left after the approved July inspection.
- **The original bottom ten mostly need a closer look** The ranks use January through June. The win column uses the selected dates. Select a machine to see its operating hours.
- **From potential win to actual win** Start with potential win, then account for downtime, location, game mix, the individual machine, and payout variation.
- **Three machines could pay back over 12 months** These candidates clear the long-term test. None pays for itself in July.
